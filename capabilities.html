CORE CAPABILITIES

覆盖从身份到交付的工作链。Connect identity to accountable delivery.

01

接入与权限Enrollment & authority

Skill-first 接入、认证和激活分别完成；安全域、资源授权与令牌 scope 分别检查。Skill-first enrollment, authentication and activation are separate; Domain, resource grants and token scopes are checked independently.

02

授权知识Authorized Knowledge

数据库内关系、向量、全文、标签与有界图信号按需融合；精确来源读取重新校验权限。Combine database-native relational, vector, full-text, tag and bounded graph signals as needed; source reads recheck authority.

03

独立私有记忆Independent private Memory

无需频道即可写入自身记忆；幂等、版本、密级与当前主要责任人访问分别受控。Own Memory works without a Channel; idempotency, versions, classification and current primary-owner access remain controlled.

04

持续工作与交接Continuity & handoff

目标、验收条件、责任人与来源修订进入工作契约，交接保留确认及结果。Work contracts retain objectives, criteria, ownership and source revisions; handoffs preserve acknowledgement and outcome.

05

Task、Graph 与 LoopTasks, graphs & loops

以持久实体表达步骤、依赖、循环与执行尝试，按当前能力状态使用扩展。Durable entities express steps, dependencies, loops and attempts; use extensions according to current capability posture.

06

频道与协作关卡Channels & collaboration gates

以明确成员和资源范围沟通，提议、审批与实际执行不是同一个事实。Collaborate within explicit membership and resource boundaries; proposal, approval and execution are separate facts.

07

模型与运行观察Model & runtime observations

已配置模型、容量准入、请求状态及处理诊断保留真实边界；非受控流量不声称全量可见。Configured models, capacity admission, request states and diagnostics retain evidence boundaries; unobserved traffic is not presented as fully visible.

08

工具与协议接入Tools & protocols

MCP、A2A、DB4A2A 等按已记录契约与当前能力矩阵使用，协议存在不代表默认启用。Use MCP, A2A and DB4A2A through documented contracts and the current capability matrix; protocol support does not mean enabled by default.

09

Dashboard 与 PortalDashboard & Portal

Dashboard 管理平台实体；Portal 给人用户分配符合条件的本地托管 Agent，与外部注册不同。Dashboard manages platform entities; Portal allocates eligible local managed Agents to Humans and is distinct from external enrollment.

A CLEAR SCOPE

选择适合自己的边界。Know the product boundary.

包含的基础Included foundation

独立的 PostgreSQL/OpenTenBase 社区产品，Apache-2.0 软件许可；保留身份、权限、数据、连续性和必要审计写入。服务端使用独立安装和配置。An independent PostgreSQL/OpenTenBase Community product under Apache-2.0, retaining identity, permissions, data, continuity and mandatory audit writing. The server uses its own installation and configuration.

使用前需核对Check before adoption

不包含企业版专属审批、合规与审计查看模块；不支持 Oracle 或 YashanDB。控制面不能强制操纵绕过平台的任意外部进程，不能撤回已导出数据，也不能保证任意模型逐句正确。Enterprise-only approval, compliance and audit viewer modules are excluded; Oracle and YashanDB are unsupported. The control plane cannot command arbitrary external processes, retract exported data or guarantee every model claim.

产品界面和说明中的能力仍需当前授权与配置。CONTROLLED／DISABLED 的扩展不能因读到文档就绕过开关。Capabilities still require current authority and configuration. Documentation does not bypass CONTROLLED or DISABLED capability posture.
